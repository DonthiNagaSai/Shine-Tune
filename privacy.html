Privacy Policy

Shine & Tune · Last updated 14 November 2026

In short: Shine & Tune collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. Your game progress is saved on your own device and never leaves it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, transmit or store any personal information. It has no sign-in, no profile and no way to contact other players. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress — coins, which shops you have built, upgrade levels, your chosen language and your sound settings — in your device's local browser storage inside the app. This data:

  • stays on your device and is never sent anywhere,
  • is not readable by us or by anyone else,
  • is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app declares a single Android permission, INTERNET, which the app container requires in order to load the game's own files. The game itself works fully offline and does not use that permission to send or receive any data.

Third-party services

This version of the app contains no advertising networks, no analytics or crash reporting services and no social media integrations. No third party receives any data through this app.

If a future version adds any such service, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.

Children

Shine & Tune is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct, export or delete. You can remove everything the app has stored by clearing its data or uninstalling it.
